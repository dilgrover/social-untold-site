Connect the Social Untold TikTok account

Social Untold Publisher posts Social Untold's own short videos (UK careers and desi life in the UK) to the Social Untold TikTok account on a schedule. Only the channel owner uses it.

How it works

  1. Connect: the owner signs in with TikTok and approves access (Login Kit, user.info.basic).
  2. Schedule: each finished video and its caption are added to the Social Untold publishing queue.
  3. Post: at the planned time the publisher uploads the video to the connected account (Content Posting API, video.upload and video.publish), with TikTok's privacy and interaction settings, and marks AI-generated content.
