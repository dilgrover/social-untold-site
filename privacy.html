Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers Social Untold Publisher, a private publishing tool operated by Social Untold (contact: dilgrover@gmail.com). It is used only to post Social Untold's own videos to Social Untold's own accounts.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to, and read and reply to comments on, the Social Untold channel.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the Social Untold Instagram account and Facebook Page, and to read and reply to comments on them.
  • TikTok: basic profile information of the Social Untold account and permission to upload and publish its videos.

How it is used

Access is used solely to publish content that Social Untold created and to manage comments on that content. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in a private repository and in encrypted CI secrets, accessible only to the Social Untold owner. Publishing logs record post IDs, links and timestamps only.

Viewers and commenters

The tool does not collect personal data about viewers. Public comments may be read and replied to through each platform's own tools, under that platform's privacy policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions page, Meta business integrations settings, or TikTok's app management settings. To request deletion of any stored data, email dilgrover@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Google API Services

Social Untold Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
